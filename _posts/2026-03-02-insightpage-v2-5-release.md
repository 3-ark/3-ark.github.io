We are excited to announce the release of **InsightPage v2.5**!

This milestone update brings major improvements to search accuracy, local model integration, and note synchronization.

## Highlights of v2.5

### 1. BM25 + Semantic Hybrid RAG
By combining keyword matching (BM25) with semantic embeddings and MMR reranking, InsightPage can now locate exact terms and conceptual matches across thousands of saved notes in milliseconds.

### 2. Native Support for On-Device Models
You can now connect InsightPage to local model runners like Ollama or LM Studio with zero data leaving your machine.

Read the full documentation in our [InsightPage Docs]({{ '/docs/insightpage/getting-started/' | relative_url }}).
