Welcome to our combined product wiki and blog repository!

This site serves two main purposes:
1. **Product Documentation (`/docs/`)**: Comprehensive user guides, architecture specifications, and API documentation for our products like **InsightPage Assistant**.
2. **Product Blog (`/blog/`)**: Engineering updates, product announcements, and technical deep dives.

## How This Wiki Works

This wiki is hosted directly on **GitHub Pages** powered by Jekyll. Markdown files placed in `docs/` automatically form the documentation tree, while articles placed in `_posts/` automatically populate the product blog.
